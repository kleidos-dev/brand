## In one paragraph

> Kleidos is free firmware that turns one of the small ESP32 boards it runs on, from M5Stack and LILYGO, into a hardware password manager. Your passwords stay encrypted on the board, which types the one you pick into your computer or phone over Bluetooth, and only after you have unlocked it on the board itself.

> Kleidos es un firmware libre y gratuito que convierte una de las pequeñas placas ESP32 en las que funciona, de M5Stack y LILYGO, en un gestor de contraseñas por hardware. Tus contraseñas se quedan cifradas en la placa, que escribe por Bluetooth en tu ordenador o tu móvil la que elijas, y solo después de que hayas desbloqueado la placa en la propia placa.

The facts, what not to write, and how to write the name: [`en/fact-sheet.md`](en/fact-sheet.md), [`es/fact-sheet.md`](es/fact-sheet.md). Longer and shorter texts: [`../about/`](../about/).

## The photograph

Four of the boards on a table, each running Kleidos: the M5Stack Cardputer ADV and the M5Stack Core2 v1.3 on the list of credentials, the M5StickS3 on one credential, the LILYGO T-Deck Plus asking for the passphrase. It is the project author's own photograph, given for publication on 2026-11-18, under the same license and the same press permission as the other images. **The screens show an earlier build of Kleidos**, so a detail on them (a version number, a minimum length, a label) may differ from the firmware today; the entries are demo data. The boards keep their makers' print, which is their owners'.

Every file is a JPEG in sRGB with no camera data: no EXIF, no location, no camera, no time. Each carries only what it is, in XMP and in IPTC: its title, description and alt text, the author, the copyright line and the license with its URL, and that it is a photograph. The full-resolution file is the only press-kit picture over 1 MB; its two narrower copies are under it. Each JPEG file also comes as a WebP file of the same name, encoded at quality 90 from the full-resolution pixels (resized as its JPEG is), with the same metadata in XMP and no camera data either.
