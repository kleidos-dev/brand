# The logo

The Kleidos mark is a keyhole inside its ring. It is always drawn whole: the keyhole never appears without the ring, at any size. Under 24 px it is drawn as the small mark: the same ring, with the body solid in the ring's color and the keyhole cut out of it. The name beside or under it is one fixed piece of lettering.

The name is always one color: white (`#eceef1`) on a dark ground, ink (`#0c0f13`) on a light one. Color comes only from the mark.

## How the files are named

- `kleidos-mark`: the mark alone. `kleidos-lockup-horizontal`: the mark and the name side by side, the primary lockup. `kleidos-lockup-stacked`: the mark above the name, for a square or tall space. `kleidos-lettering`: the name alone, for a layout where the mark already stands nearby (a profile whose picture is the mark); it never replaces the mark.
- `-for-dark`, `-for-light`: on transparency, with the name in the ink that reads on that ground. The mark keeps its colors on both.
- `-on-dark`, `-on-light`: the same on a baked ground (`#0a0c0f` or white), with the clear space included.
- `-ink-dark`, `-ink-light`: the whole logo in one ink, dark or light, the body's fill left out. These are official variants for one-color print and engraving, not a recoloring anyone is invited to make.
- `kleidos-mark-small-<px>`: the small mark, fitted to one size, the last number of both its SVG and its PNG. Each is drawn for its own pixels: use the file of the size the mark is shown at, never one scaled to another size.
- A PNG's last number is its height in pixels.

## How it is built

`src/mark.svg` is the only drawing kept by hand: five shapes on a 100-unit box (a ring of radius 42 and stroke 3; a body of 28 by 56 with a radius of 14, filled and outlined at 2.5; a bit of radius 5; a stem of 3 by 14). The name is "Kleidos" set in Mona Sans at width 112.5, weight 650, optical size 72, tracked in by 0.02 em, shaped once and kept as one path. `src/lockups.json` holds the lockups' numbers in ems of that lettering:

| Lockup | Mark box | Lettering | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Horizontal | 1.32 em, left | baseline 0.364 em below the mark's center; origin 0.32 em right of the mark box | settled: the numbers of the organization profile's banner |
| Stacked | 2.6 em, above | centered on the mark's axis by its ink; cap top 0.3 em under the mark box | settled: approved by the owner on 2026-11-17 |

`src/small-mark.json` holds the small mark's sizes (19, 21 and 23 px, odd so the keyhole stands on one pixel column), the size it applies under (24 px) and its pixel rules. `tools/kleidos_brand/mark.py` fits it from the mark's own numbers: the ring's outer diameter is the file's side, and the body's width, its height, the eye's height and the stem's length are the mark's, scaled to that diameter and rounded to whole, odd numbers of pixels, so every edge falls on a pixel's edge and the keyhole stands on one pixel column. The ring and the stem are one pixel wide, the eye a dot of three pixels with its corners softened, the body keeps at least a pixel of solid either side of the eye and a pixel of space inside the ring. The body and the keyhole are one path, the keyhole a hole in it, so the ground shows through the keyhole and the mark has a single color. A small mark's PNG is not drawn by the browser: each pixel's alpha is the share of its 16 by 16 sample points that fall inside those shapes, so the file is the same on every machine.

| Small mark | Ring | Body | Eye, stem |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 px | 19 px | 7 by 13 | 3 px dot, 1 by 3 below its center |
| 21 px | 21 px | 7 by 13 | 3 px dot, 1 by 3 below its center |
| 23 px | 23 px | 9 by 17 | 3 px dot, 1 by 4 below its center |

Everything under `svg/` and `png/` is written by `tools/build.py`; change the sources, never those files.

## Clear space and smallest sizes

- Clear space: half the mark box (the ring's radius, near enough) on every side of the mark or a lockup. The `-on-dark` and `-on-light` files already include it. A profile picture is the exception: there the ring may fill the crop.
- On a dark ground the full mark holds down to 24 px of mark box on a screen, and 6 mm in print.
- Under 24 px, use the small mark (`kleidos-mark-small-<px>`), at 19, 21 or 23 px, and from 24 px the full mark; nothing smaller than 19 px is drawn. A small mark's file is its ring, edge to edge, with no clear space inside it: keep half its side free around it, except where the file is the whole square (a favicon, a slot in an app's header). On a dark ground use the color file; on a light one, the one-ink `-ink-dark` or the dark tile `-on-dark`, since the small mark in color has no light-ground file.
- On a light ground the color mark holds from 48 px: its ring is 1.80:1 on white, so the body carries it, and below 48 px the ring thins to a hairline. Under that size use the mark on its dark tile (`-on-dark`) or the one-ink mark (`-ink-dark`).
- At every size the mark keeps its ring. The small mark is the whole mark fitted to pixels, never the keyhole alone.
- Against a left margin, the ring's outer edge stands on the margin, not the mark box: the box keeps 6.5 % of its side free around the ring, which would set the mark in from the lines under it. The 16:9 press layouts place the lockup that way.

## Using it

What follows is a request. It adds no condition to the license (see [`../TRADEMARKS.md`](../TRADEMARKS.md), which also holds the permission that lets anyone republish these files as they are).

Please do:

- use a file as it is, and scale it in proportion;
- put it on the brand's dark ground, black, white, or a calm area of a photograph, with the clear space kept.

Please do not:

- show the keyhole without its ring;
- scale a small mark's file to another size, or use the small mark from 24 px up;
- redraw, recolor, outline, tilt, stretch or crop the mark, or animate its parts apart;
- add a shadow, a glow or a gradient;
- set the name in another face as part of the logo (in running text, and on the device's own screens, the name is simply written in the text's face);
- place the color mark where its ring falls under 3:1 against the ground, unless the one-ink file is used;
- combine it with another mark as if they were one;
- print the registered-trademark sign beside it: Kleidos is not a registered trademark.

The PNG files carry no text, so they cannot say this themselves: the license of every file here is GPL-3.0-or-later, with the additional terms of `TRADEMARKS.md`.
